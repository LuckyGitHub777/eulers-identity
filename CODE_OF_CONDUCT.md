# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants must communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people,
and make space for learners with different levels of mathematical experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, deliberate
misrepresentation of another contributor's work, intimidation, and publishing
private information without permission.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code
review, and project-related public communication.

## Reporting

For immediate platform abuse or a concern requiring confidentiality, use
GitHub's **Report abuse** controls.

For a non-sensitive project-specific concern, open an issue containing only the
information needed to understand the concern. Do not publish private or
sensitive information.

The project does not advertise a private conduct channel until a project-level
channel has been configured and verified.

Project moderators may remove content, limit participation, close discussions,
or block accounts when necessary to protect the project and its participants.
