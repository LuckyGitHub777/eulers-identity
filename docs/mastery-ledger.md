## Meaning of status

- **Complete** — implemented and covered by the stated evidence
- **Foundation only** — enough for the current release, not the full mastery gate
- **Pending** — not represented as complete
- **Released** — technical foundation release published through protected GitHub gates


## Validation policy

Learner observation and manual screen-reader or physical-device review are declared
`0.1.x` validation activities. They do not block the technical `0.1.0`
foundation release, but they are required before the project may claim the
complete mastery experience at `1.0.0`.
