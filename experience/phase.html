Add two equal amplitudes

The first phasor is fixed at phase 0. Change the second phasor’s relative phase from 0 to 2π. At π, the two equal amplitudes point in opposite directions and their vector sum is exactly zero.

What is happening?

At zero relative phase, the equal phasors reinforce and the magnitude is 2.

1 + e^(i·0) = 2

Evidence boundary

This laboratory demonstrates addition of equal complex amplitudes. Cancellation at relative phase π is exact mathematics. A physical interference experiment additionally depends on the physical system, amplitudes, dynamics, and measurement context.
